Who We Are

About Greater Works Parish

Region 11 · Lagos Province 6 · Zone 2 · Area 2 — part of the Redeemed Christian Church of God.

Our Story

Greater Works Parish is a local parish of the Redeemed Christian Church of God, planted to be a house of prayer, deep teaching, and genuine community in Ajah, Lagos. Our name comes from John 14:12 — a daily reminder that the works God does through His people are meant to grow, not shrink.

More of our history will be added here soon.

Our Vision

To raise a people who know God's word deeply, walk in His presence daily, and carry His works beyond the walls of the church.
